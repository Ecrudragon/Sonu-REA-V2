# Responsible use and scope

This repository is educational material, not legal advice. You are responsible for confirming that your activities are lawful and authorized.

## Authorization

Analyze only software you own or for which you have explicit permission from the owner. Establish written scope before testing, including the app and version, permitted techniques, test accounts and devices, data handling, time window, and stop conditions. Do not access private app data or systems outside that scope.

## Safety and privacy

- Prefer source code and vendor-provided test builds.
- Use isolated test devices, non-production accounts, and synthetic data where practical.
- Keep original artifacts and record versions and checksums for reproducibility.
- Do not publish APKs, credentials, signing keys, private certificates, personal data, or confidential findings.
- Do not bypass payment, authentication, licensing, integrity protections, or other access controls.
- Do not alter or redistribute third-party software without the necessary permission.

## Reporting

Share suspected vulnerabilities privately with the software owner through its published security contact or coordinated disclosure program. Provide enough information to help verify and remediate the issue, but avoid exposing users or sharing exploit material publicly before coordination.
